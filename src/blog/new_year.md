# Happy New Year
## It's 2026 {#2026}

The new year is upon us. It's 2026, it will be an important year for me, a lot will happen health-wise, which I hope will help my chronic pain. 

I didn't explain a lot about my disabilities here, but I have chronic pain and chronic fatigue (CFS). Among other things. 

It killed my academic career and forced me to find a job at the end of my bachelor's degree, but I accepted it and I'm happy with how it turned out. 

## Year of Health {#health}

I remember CGP Gray's videos on new year's wishes. I kind of agree, and in his spirit I think the main topic of 2026 will be Health.

I need to take care of myself, I learned that the hard way on the past few months. 

## Year of... Going out? {#goingout}

I don't want my year to be all about health though, I'm approaching medical burnout. 

And for external problems I decided I needed friends, and potentially a partner. Thus I decided to go out more. 

Some weeks I'll go out to a meeting of queer people, some weeks I'll try to go out in quiet bars, some weeks I'll just rest at home. 

I also want to find a TTRPG group I can join.

## Afterwords {#afterwords}

Happy New Year everyone! May 2026 bring happiness on you. 

I hope I'll have a good year, I think I'm gonna have a good year, well I'm mostly hoping. 

Best wishes, 
Cheers,

Charlotte Thomas.
